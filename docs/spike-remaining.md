When Photos access is denied or restricted, imports go to Files whatever the setting. It is a scope addition to Iteration 1: a settings control and the Import screen's wording (which destination, and why when forced).
- **Files folder layout:** one folder per camera, named for the camera (for example its model name), matching the album default. A name already present is never overwritten.
- **Production App ID:** a new App ID and App Store Connect record, bundle ID `net.shamil.shutter-bridge`, record named "Shutter Bridge". Shamil creates them on the Mac before the first upload (Iteration 2's TestFlight). The spike keeps its own.
- **Video files in Browse:** shown greyed out with a "Video: from 1.1" label, not importable in 1.0.
- **Photos `.restricted`** (parental controls or device management) is treated as denied: imports go to Files and the app says why, without the Settings link. After someone grants Photos access, the next import follows the destination setting, and files already in Files stay where they are, still marked imported. Files written to Files get the camera's capture date as their modification date (the bytes stay the originals).

## 5. Design decisions for 1.0

Each was decided by Shamil: decision 2 on 4 Nov, the rest on 7 Nov, as recommended. The recommendations are drawn from the findings; a "Provisional on" line names a run or an answer in sections 2 and 3 that could still refine the detail.

**1. USB: the 30 s foreground limit.**
- *Evidence:* in MSC, ImageCaptureCore reports *will suspend* when the app leaves the foreground and *did suspend* 30.0–30.1 s later, in the background (five times of five: four at 01:57, one at 11:56) and under lock (03:07; nothing moved for the remaining 629 s). A continued task doesn't change it. The session survives, and *resumed operations* comes 0.3–0.9 s after the app returns. A full card of 360 ARW takes about 6½ minutes at 40 MB/s. MTP behaves the same (4 Nov 11:30, three of three). In *Remote Shoot (PC Remote)* ImageCaptureCore lists no files (12:10).
- *Recommendation:* the import asks to stay in front and keeps the screen on; the engine pauses on *did suspend* and continues when the app returns. A continued task over USB buys at most 30 s, so it isn't a requirement for wired. In PC Remote ImageCaptureCore lists no files, so the app asks for MSC or MTP there.
- *Provisional on:* nothing pending (PC Remote answered 4 Nov 12:10).
- *Decision (Shamil, 7 Nov):* accepted as recommended.

**2. Wi-Fi: leave the camera's network after an import, or stay joined.**
- *Evidence:* in two runs (3 Nov 22:43, 4 Nov 13:06), removing the hotspot configuration 45–46 s after CloseSession made the camera's Wi-Fi start switching off 51.0–51.2 s later and be off at 53.0–53.2 s, with no write. A third leave (5 Nov 01:13; Creators' App open, as it was at 22:43) didn't switch it off within the 59 s watched, so the switch-off after a leave isn't always within a minute. Removing it brings the iOS join prompt back; re-applying a saved configuration (the 01:12 recovery) didn't prompt. If the phone stays joined, the camera's Wi-Fi stays up: status 2 for at least 785 s after CloseSession on 4 Nov, through background spells and a 73 s lock, with the app's Bluetooth link up and Sony's notifications on throughout (with the link closed, only 63–64 s is tested), and the phone's internet on mobile data meanwhile. Creators' App's Wi-Fi goes off about 1 s after it leaves; how isn't known. The camera's battery cost of a Wi-Fi left on isn't measured.
- *Options:* (a) leave after each import: a join prompt every time, the camera's Wi-Fi off about 53 s later in two runs of three (still on 59 s after the 5 Nov leave); (b) stay joined: no prompt, but the camera's Wi-Fi stays on while the phone is joined (at least 13 minutes measured) and the phone's internet goes over mobile data meanwhile; (c) (b) plus the write, if the trace finds it (closed for 1.0, 7 Nov: the trace was skipped); (d) stay joined for a few minutes after an import, so a second import needs no prompt, then leave (the app has to be running then to remove its configuration; doing that from the background is untested).
- *Decision (Shamil, 4 Nov):* (d). (b) alone would leave the camera's Wi-Fi on and the phone on mobile data while they're together (at least 13 minutes measured; longer, and the battery cost, untested). Still to settle in Iteration 3, by Shamil: how long to stay, and leaving with the app in the background (settled 8 Nov, below).
- *Decision (Shamil, 7 Nov):* (d) stands. The Bluetooth trace of Creators' App leaving was skipped, so decision 2 rests on the measured behaviour and (c) is closed for 1.0. The write that makes the camera's Wi-Fi go off at once after a leave stays unknown.
- *Decision (Shamil, 8 Nov):* option (a), as recommended in the decision pack. One stay timer from the end of the import, running only while the app is in front, default 3 minutes in a single tunable constant; leave at once in the short background time if the app goes to the background after the import; an import that finishes in the background leaves as the continued task's last step; on a return to the front, leave if the stay has run out; keep the Bluetooth link up during the stay and reconnect if it drops, else leave or shorten the stay. Leaving from the background is tested in Iteration 3, week 1.

**3. Wi-Fi: join promptly, and recover.**
- *Evidence:* the camera gives about 50 s from status 2 (*terminating* 50.0 s after status 2, seven runs of seven; with the Bluetooth link closed it was off when read 77 s after status 2; once, with Creators' App open, the Wi-Fi and the link went after about 30 s, 3 Nov 11:54); the joins took 7.4–10.6 s with the prompt. In the 4 Nov recovery, two fixed 30 s waits made up 62 of 97 s. The second was waiting for iOS to rejoin; the first was waiting for the port. Only the join is known to need the foreground.
- *Recommendation:* confirm a join by the camera's gateway on the Wi-Fi path, not by `apply` returning, and try again if the gateway doesn't come (at 12:34 on 4 Nov `apply` returned without an error and the join failed; the next attempt, with nothing changed, joined; the first join after a camera restart worked first time on 5 Nov, so a restart alone doesn't explain it). Join as soon as CC09 reports status 2; watch CC09 during the join prompt and, if it reaches 3 or 0, write CC08 = 0x01 again and retry. Don't count on the Bluetooth link after the join: on 5 Nov it dropped by itself 11 s after one (a CoreBluetooth internal error), so the app reconnects when it next needs CC09. Removing the second wait is measured (join as soon as CC09 reports 2). The first wait, before going to Bluetooth, may have to stay: the app can't yet tell a camera that is back on from one the person switched off, and 1.0 shouldn't switch on the latter. Keep a wait, and treat how to tell them apart as open. A join needs the app in front. Apple's hotspot API has an error for an app that isn't in the foreground (`applicationIsNotInForeground`), and the spike's recovery doesn't try the join unless the app is in front. At 01:08, with the home network in range, iOS didn't rejoin the camera's network by itself in 30 s (with no other known network in range: untested). So with the app away, the copy does what needs no join: it waits for the card, reconnects, switches the camera's Wi-Fi on over Bluetooth if allowed, and waits for iOS to rejoin. If a join is still needed, it pauses and notifies, and joins when the app returns. The spike's copy ends at that point instead of pausing, so this pause is new in 1.0.
- *Provisional on:* nothing pending: the locked-recovery run was dropped (Shamil, 4 Nov). Left unmeasured: a Bluetooth switch-on from the background, and whether iOS rejoins by itself while the phone is locked.
- *Decision (Shamil, 7 Nov):* accepted as recommended.

**4. Pause, continue and stalled files.**
- *Evidence:* both Wi-Fi expiries came as the Camera app was opened. The three USB expiries came 31–74 s after the progress last moved, during ImageCaptureCore's suspension: two in the 01:57 copy, phone unlocked, with what was in front not recorded, and one in the 03:07 run with the phone locked (Stop in the Live Activity not ruled out). Four more, three in the 11:30 MTP copy (34–59 s) and one in the 11:56 MSC copy (36 s), came after the progress stopped, phone unlocked, with Stop ruled out by Shamil; yet in the 01:57 copy three stalls went about 60 s without progress without one (two of them were 109–114 s without a byte, with a failed file moving the progress at 60 s). *Continue* started a new task in 0.03 s and copied again 11 s after the tap, 8.3 s of it a relist that stable handles make unnecessary; the file in flight restarted from its start. Over USB the same `requestDownload` survived a 632 s suspension, yet the spike's 60 s limit failed three downloads. Over Wi-Fi a drop made the copy count 82 files as failed before the link closed.
- *Recommendation:* treat expiry as normal: pause, notify, and on Continue start a new task, reopen, ask for new handles only, and resume at the file in flight. Report progress in bytes. A stall pauses and retries; "failed" is for real errors, with a limited number of retries. A cable pull or other loss of the camera pauses the copy too, without failing the file in flight. Once the camera is back and listed again (11.3 s for 549 files in MSC, 65–123 s for 391–825 files in MTP), the copy carries on: it finds the files left by identity (decision 7) and copies the file in flight again from its start. In the 11:56 run the spike's copy ended instead, and the file in flight was counted as failed.
- *Decision (Shamil, 7 Nov):* accepted as recommended.
- *Decision (Shamil, 8 Nov), when to ask for notification permission:* option (b). Once, at the first *Import* on either link, with a one-line reason; the reminder switch also asks if permission is still undetermined; from whichever iteration first posts a pause notification.

- *Onboarding:* one pairing from the app, with the camera's Bluetooth *Pairing* screen open; Creators' App isn't needed.
- *Decision (Shamil, 7 Nov):* accepted as recommended.

The iPhone's pairing is shared: Creators' App, added after ours, reused it with no prompt, both apps worked (5 Nov 00:58–01:25), and the pairing survived two camera restarts (01:18 and 02:21). **7. Wired mode, identity and resume.**

Identity: `PersistentUniqueObjectIdentifier` in MTP (about 385 ms per object, so read it only for new objects; MTP handles were also stable across a replug); folder, name, capture date and size in MSC. Ask ImageCaptureCore for thumbnails two at a time (73.0 ms each in MTP, against 80.8 ms one at a time and 87.6 ms four at a time; one run each; MSC not measured); in MTP, don't read previews from the files (369–435 ms each; MSC, where the card is a mounted file system, untested) unless the grid needs more than ImageCaptureCore's thumbnail, most likely the camera's 160×120.
- *Provisional on:* nothing pending. The *MSC: resume after a cable pull* run was dropped on 4 Nov (Shamil): an ARW restarts in about 1 s, and the 11:56 cable pull showed what a copy does. In MSC the card is a mounted file system, so folder and name shouldn't change across a replug; that isn't tested after a replug.
- *Decision (Shamil, 7 Nov):* accepted as recommended.
